El superlativo

2. Es la ciudad más poblada de las tres.
7. Es la ciudad más antigua de las tres.

These two sentences use the superlative: a form we use to say that someone or something has the highest degree of a quality in a group.

Connect with English

The superlative in English

big → bigger →
the biggest
old → older →
the oldest
interesting → more interesting →
the most interesting
populated → more populated →
the most populated
English can use -est or the most + adjective. Spanish normally uses más + adjective.
The pattern

How do we form the superlative?

el / la / los / las + (noun) + más + adjective + (de + group)
el / la / los / las

The definite article agrees with the noun.
(noun)

The noun can be omitted when it is clear from the context.
(de + group)

Shows the group or range of comparison. It can be omitted when the group is already clear.

The parts in parentheses are optional when the information is already clear from the context.

Look at the textbook sentence

Building the structure

la + ciudad + más + poblada + de las tres
la ciudad más poblada de las tres
the most populated city of the three
de las tres tells us the group being compared: the three cities.
The noun can disappear

When the noun is clear

Madrid es la ciudad más grande.
Madrid es la más grande.

Both sentences are possible. In the second sentence, ciudad is omitted because we already know that we are talking about cities.

The article remains: la más grande. The article still agrees with the omitted noun: la ciudad.
The comparison group can disappear

When the group is already clear

¿Cuál es la ciudad más grande de España?

Madrid es la más grande.

We do not need to repeat de España, because the comparison group is already clear from the question.

de + group gives the group or range of comparison, but it does not always need to be stated.
Adjective agreement

The adjective still agrees with the noun

el país más poblado
la ciudad más poblada
los países más poblados
las ciudades más pobladas
MÁS never changes. The adjective agrees with the noun in gender and number.

Summary

The superlative

el / la / los / las + (noun) + más + adjective + (de + group)
(noun) → can be omitted when it is clear.
(de + group) → gives the group or range of comparison; it can be omitted when the group is already clear.
más never changes; the adjective agrees with the noun.
