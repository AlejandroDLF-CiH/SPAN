usted uses the same form as él / ella. ustedes uses the same form as ellos / ellas.

New idea

Three types of Spanish verbs

hablAR

to speak

comER

to eat

vivIR

to live

To conjugate a regular verb, we remove -ar, -er or -ir and add a new ending.

How it works

Conjugation = stem + ending

hablar
→
habl-
+
-o
=
hablo

hablo = I speak

Step 1: find the infinitive ending. Step 2: remove it. Step 3: add the correct person ending.

Compare the three types

What is the same?

yo → -o for all three types.

What is very similar?

-ER and -IR are almost the same.

The main difference between -er and -ir is in nosotros and vosotros: -emos / -imos and -éis / -ís.

Important

Regular vs irregular verbs

Regular verbs

hablar → hablo

comer → como

vivir → vivo

Irregular verbs

ser → soy

tener → tengo

Not all Spanish verbs are regular. Today, focus on the regular pattern. We will learn irregular verbs step by step.
