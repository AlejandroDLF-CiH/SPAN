What does querer mean?

querer
= to want
English
I want to learn Spanish.
Spanish
Quiero aprender español.

In Spanish, querer is a verb.

It changes according to the subject.
Page 2

Conjugation of querer

Subject querer
yo quiero
tú quieres
él / ella / usted quiere
nosotros / nosotras queremos
vosotros / vosotras queréis
ellos / ellas / ustedes quieren
Stem change:
e → ie
nosotros and vosotros do not change:
queremos, queréis
Page 3

Querer + infinitive

want to + verb → querer + infinitive
I want to learn Spanish.
Quiero aprender español.
Quiero
conjugated verb
aprender
infinitive
Only querer changes. The second verb stays in the infinitive.
Quieres comer.
You want to eat.
Queremos viajar.
We want to travel.
Page 4

English vs. Spanish

English Spanish
I want to learn Spanish. Quiero aprender español.
You want to eat. Quieres comer.
She wants to travel. Quiere viajar.
We want to learn Spanish. Queremos aprender español.
They want to live in Spain. Quieren vivir en España.

English

I want to learn Spanish.
She wants to learn Spanish.

Spanish

quiero, quieres, quiere
queremos, queréis, quieren
Spanish verbs change according to the subject.
