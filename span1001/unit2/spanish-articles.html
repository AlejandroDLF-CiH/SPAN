What’s an article?

the book
a book

In English, we use the as the definite article, and a / an as indefinite articles.

Spanish also has definite and indefinite articles.

But Spanish articles change according to gender and number.

Page 5

Definite or Indefinite?

What’s the difference between el libro and un libro?

un libro

one book, not specific

el libro

a specific book

Tengo un libro.
El libro es interesante.
First, we introduce a book. Then we talk about the same book.
