Part 1 · Start with the title

What do I have to take?

A familiar verb, with a new meaning.

¿Qué tengo que llevar?

What do I have to take with me?

quéwhat
tengo queI have to
llevarto take (with you)

Something I have

Tengo un pasaporte.

I have a passport.

Something I have to do

Tengo que llevar el pasaporte.

I have to take my passport with me.

Tener que means “to have to”. It tells us that an action is necessary.

Part 2 · The action changes

Keep the next verb in the infinitive

An infinitive is the basic form of a verb. It ends in -ar, -er or -ir.

Choose a different action

Tengo que estudiar español.
I have to study Spanish.

Dashed underline: the action can change. The verb stays in the infinitive.

Three infinitive endings
