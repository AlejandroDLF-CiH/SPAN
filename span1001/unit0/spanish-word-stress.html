´Spanish Word Stress
Part 1 · Find the stress

Page 1 · The idea

What is word stress?

When we say a word, one syllable usually stands out. This is the stressed syllable. A syllable is a beat in a word.

CA-sa2 syllables
es-pa-ÑOL3 syllables
te-LÉ-fo-no4 syllables
Stress is something we hear. A tilde (´) is a mark we see.

Page 2 · Step 1

Look for an accent mark

Does the word have á, é, í, ó or ú?

Yes ✓

No accent mark?

Go to Step 2 and look at the final letter.

The accent mark shows you where to put the stress.

Page 3 · Step 2

Look at the ending

For most words without an accent mark:

Word ending
Stress position
Examples
A vowel (a, e, i, o, u), n, or s
Second-to-last syllable
ME-sa · LI-bro · HA-blan · LU-nes
Any other consonant
Last syllable
ha-BLAR · pa-PEL · es-pa-ÑOL

Remember: accent mark first. Word ending second.

Page 4 · Say the stress

Make one syllable stand out

Give the stressed syllable a little more emphasis.

1Say it with a little more energy.
2Hold its vowel slightly longer while practising.
3Keep the other vowels clear.
4Keep the word flowing. Do not pause.
ME-sa
pa-PEL
te-LÉ-fo-no
You do not need to shout. Tap a word and say it smoothly.
