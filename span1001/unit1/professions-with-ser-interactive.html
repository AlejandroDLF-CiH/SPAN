SPAN1001 · Professions with SER

Talking about professions

We use ser + a profession to say what someone does.

Soy profesor.

I am a teacher.

Ella es médica.

She is a doctor.

Normally, we do not use un / una when we simply state someone's profession.

Soy profesor. ✓
