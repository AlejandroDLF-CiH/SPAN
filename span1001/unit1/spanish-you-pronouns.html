SPAN1001 · SPANISH SUBJECT PRONOUNS

01 · THE BIG PICTURE

Different ways to say “you”

In English, you can mean one person or a group. Spanish has different forms.

ONE PERSON · SINGULAR
tú · vos · usted

You are speaking to one person.

TWO OR MORE PEOPLE · PLURAL
vosotros · vosotras
ustedes

You are speaking to a group.

They all mean “you”. The form depends on the number of people, the situation and the region.

02 · SPAIN & MANY PARTS OF LATIN AMERICA

Talking to one person: tú and usted

In Spain and many parts of Latin America, people use tú and usted when speaking to one person.

tú

Informal

With friends, classmates and people you know well.

Informal does not mean rude.

usted

Formal

To show respect or keep some distance.

For example, in a formal first meeting.

túusted
Tú eres estudiante.Usted es estudiante.
Tú tienes veinte años.Usted tiene veinte años.
Usted means “you”, but uses the same verb forms as él / ella: es, tiene.

These are common patterns. Local habits and your relationship also matter. A teacher or an older person may use tú with you.

03 · ARGENTINA, PARAGUAY & URUGUAY

Another way to say “you”: vos

ArgentinaParaguayUruguay

vos is very common

It means one person’s “you” and is common with friends and people you know well.

It is a normal local form of Spanish.

usted is also used

People also use usted, for example in formal conversations.

In Uruguay, tú is also used. Local patterns vary.

Verbtúvos
sertú eresvos sos
tenertú tienesvos tenés
Same meaning: “You are a student.”Tú eres estudiante.
Vos sos estudiante.
Same meaning: “You are twenty.”Tú tienes veinte años.
Vos tenés veinte años.

Learn to recognise these common forms first. Vos is also used in other parts of Latin America.

04 · YOUR LEARNING GOALS

What do you need to learn now?

For speaking to one person, focus on tú and usted in this beginner course.

LEARN TO USE
tú · usted

Practise these forms

Learn when to use them and learn their verb forms.

tú eresusted es
tú tienesusted tiene
RECOGNISE ONLY
vos

Just know what it means

If you see or hear vos, recognise it as “you” for one person.

Remember that it is common in Argentina, Paraguay and Uruguay.

It is also used in other parts of Latin America.

At this beginner stage, you do not need to use vos or memorise its verb forms.
You do not need to learn to produce forms such as sos or tenés. Recognising vos is enough.

This is our learning priority for now. Vos is a normal and widely used regional form of Spanish.

05 · MOST OF SPAIN · INFORMAL GROUPS

Vosotros or vosotras?

In most of Spain, use these forms when speaking to a group of friends.

vosotros

A group of men or boys,
or a mixed group.

Two male classmates

vosotras

A group of women or girls only.

Two female classmates

A male friend + a female friend

vosotros
Remember the region: this is the common informal group pattern in most of Spain.

06 · A GROUP · REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

When do we use ustedes?

Ustedes means “you” when speaking to two or more people.

MOST OF SPAIN

Formal groups

Use ustedes in a formal conversation with a group.

SPANISH-SPEAKING LATIN AMERICA

All groups

Use ustedes in both formal and informal situations.

Same situation: talking to a group of friends

Madrid
Common local usage

vosotros / vosotras

Mexico City or Buenos Aires

ustedes
Ustedes works for male, female and mixed groups. In Latin America, the group form is ustedes whether the singular form is tú, vos or usted.

Regional note: informal ustedes is also common in the Canary Islands and parts of western Andalusia.
