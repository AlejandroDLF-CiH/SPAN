Capital lettersCountry names begin with a capital letter. Nationalities and languages normally begin with a lowercase letter.

Masculine & feminineNationality adjectives agree with the person: chino / china. Some stay the same: canadiense.

Language names stay the sameBoth a man and a woman say Hablo chino.

The languages listed are examples. A country may have more than one language.
