SPANISH / REQUESTSA2–B1 · Asking & responding

01 / The basic idea

Ask for something.
Ask someone to help.

Choose the verb that fits the situation.

What do you need the other person to do?

Give you some water.

Pass you your jacket.

Lend you a car.

Serve you a coffee.

First, choose a verb. Then, make your request and listen to the reply.

02 / Choose a verb

Five useful situations

VerbWhen to use itTextbook example
darAsk someone to give you something.¿Me das otro vaso de agua, por favor?
pasarAsk someone to pass you something.¿Me pasas la chaqueta, por favor?
prestar / dejarBorrow something and return it later.¿Me prestas / dejas tu coche este fin de semana?
tenerAsk if someone has something you need.¿Tienes un bolígrafo?
ponerOrder in a café, bar or food shop.¿Me pone un cortado, por favor?

These are typical uses. Dar does not always mean “keep it forever”.

¿Tienes un bolígrafo? literally asks “Do you have a pen?” In the right situation, it can also be a way to ask to borrow one.

03 / Make the request

One useful pattern

¿Me + verb + thing + por favor?

Me means “to me” or “for me” here.
Por favor means “please”.

Speaking to tú
¿Me das / pasas / prestas…?
¿Me pones un cortado?

Speaking to usted
¿Me pone un cortado?

The waiter can also ask what you want:

¿Te pongo algo más? ¿Un café?
¿Qué le pongo?

Here, the waiter is offering to serve the customer. Te refers to tú; le refers to usted.

04 / Respond

Say yes — or explain why not.

What you want to sayUseful replies
Yes, I can help.Claro. / Sí, sin problema.
Here you are.Sí, toma.
Right away!¡Enseguida!
Sorry — here is my reason.Lo siento. Es que…

— ¿Me prestas tu coche este fin de semana?

— Uy, este fin de semana imposible. Es que lo necesito. Lo siento.

Es que… introduces the reason. Lo siento adds an apology.

05 / Practice

Choose the best verb.

Read the situation. Choose an answer, then check the feedback.

1. You want another glass of water.

¿Me ______ otro vaso de agua, por favor?

2. Your jacket is next to your friend. You want your friend to pass it to you.

¿Me ______ la chaqueta, por favor?

3. You want to borrow your friend’s car for the weekend.

¿Me ______ tu coche este fin de semana?

4. You need a pen. You do not know if your friend has one.

¿______ un bolígrafo?

5. You are ordering a coffee in a café. You are speaking to the waiter using usted.

¿Me ______ un cortado, por favor?
