How do we describe what life was like, what we used to do, and what was happening in the past?

Background Past habits Ongoing actions
Cuando era pequeño, vivía en Madrid.

Today: use it → form it → combine it with the pretérito perfecto simple (indefinido).

01 · Meaning

When do we use the imperfecto?

Three common situations.

1

Past situations

Describe what someone or something was like in the past.

Cuando era pequeño, vivía en Madrid.
When I was little, I lived in Madrid.
2

Past habits

Talk about things you used to do or did regularly.

Antes jugaba al fútbol todos los días.
I used to play football every day.
3

Ongoing past actions

Describe what was happening at a moment in the past.

Estudiaba cuando sonó el teléfono.
I was studying when the phone rang.
Remember: Imperfecto presents past situations, habits or actions in progress without focusing on their beginning or end.

03 · Contrast

Imperfecto vs. perfecto simple

The pretérito perfecto simple is also called indefinido.

Imperfecto
Background

How things were, what people used to do, or what was happening.

Antes vivía en Madrid.
Perfecto simple · indefinido
Events

What happened — actions presented as complete events or whole periods.

Ayer visité a Ana.
Important: The difference is not simply “long actions vs. short actions.” It is how the speaker presents the past.

03 · Meaning

Same verb. Different viewpoint.

Both tenses can describe living in Madrid. The perspective changes.

Imperfecto
Antes vivía en Madrid.

Describes a past situation, without focusing on its boundaries.

Perfecto simple · indefinido
Viví en Madrid tres años.

Views the three-year period as a complete stage of the past.

Ask yourself: Am I describing the past from inside the situation, or presenting an event / period as a whole?
A completed action is not necessarily short. Viví allí diez años is also possible.

05 · Takeaways

Three questions to remember

Choose a tense according to how you want to present the past.

What was it like?
↓
Imperfecto

A past situation or description.

What did you usually do?
↓
Imperfecto

A past habit or repeated activity.

What was happening when something happened?
↓
Imperfecto + indefinido

An ongoing background action and an event.
