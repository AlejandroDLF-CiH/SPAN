1 · Usage

Uses of estar + gerundio

The structure estar + gerundio is used to describe an action that is in progress at a particular moment. The verb estar is conjugated according to the subject and tense, while the gerund remains unchanged.

Estamos estudiando la historia de España este semestre.
“We are studying the history of Spain this semester.”

Usage Notes

Two important points

Notice

Spanish does not always need the progressive.

Unlike English, Spanish does not always use the progressive form for actions happening now. The simple present is often sufficient:

¿Qué haces?
“What are you doing?”
Simultaneous Action

The gerund happens at the same time as estar.

The gerund normally expresses an action that is simultaneous with the time indicated by estar.

Está cenando.
Estuvo cenando.

2 · Formation

Regular formation of the gerund

To form the gerund, remove the infinitive ending and add the appropriate ending:

Infinitive ending Gerund ending Examples
-ar -ando hablar → hablando, trabajar → trabajando
-er -iendo comer → comiendo, beber → bebiendo
-ir -iendo vivir → viviendo, escribir → escribiendo

3A · Irregular Forms

Stem-changing -ir verbs

Many -ir verbs undergo a stem-vowel change in the gerund. In general, stem-changing -ir verbs change e → i or o → u in the gerund.

e → i
pedir → pidiendo
decir → diciendo
sentir → sintiendo
venir → viniendo
o → u
dormir → durmiendo
morir → muriendo

3B & 3C · Irregular Forms

Other irregular forms

B. Verbs whose stem ends in a vowel

With many -er and -ir verbs whose stem ends in a vowel, -iendo becomes -yendo. This change avoids having three consecutive vowels and reflects the pronunciation.

leer → leyendo
oír → oyendo
creer → creyendo
traer → trayendo
caer → cayendo
construir → construyendo

C. Other frequent irregular forms

ir → yendo
