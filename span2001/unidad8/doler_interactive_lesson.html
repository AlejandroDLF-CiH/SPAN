DOLER / Spanish grammar
Interactive classroom lesson · A1–A2
01 / Meaning

DOLER

to hurt · to ache

We use doler to talk about physical pain, especially when a body part hurts.

Me duele la cabeza. My head hurts. / I have a headache.
02 / Conjugation

In expressions of physical pain, we most often use duele (singular subject) or duelen (plural subject). The other forms exist, but they are not our main focus here.
03 / Sentence structure

Who is the subject?

With doler, the body part that hurts is the grammatical subject. The person who feels the pain is expressed as an indirect object.

04 / Subject–verb agreement

Duele or duelen?

Choose the verb according to the grammatical number of the body part, not the number of people who feel pain.

DUELE
Singular subject
  • la cabeza
  • el estómago
  • la espalda
  • la garganta
DUELEN
Plural subject
  • los pies
  • los oídos
  • las muelas
  • las piernas
Me duele la cabeza.
Me duelen los pies.
Word order: The subject usually comes after the verb in this pattern. It can also appear before the verb for emphasis: La cabeza me duele.
05 / Who feels the pain?

Indirect-object pronouns

The pronoun tells us who experiences the pain. Select a person to see the correct pronoun. The body part remains the same.

Live example
Me duele la cabeza.
My head hurts.
Two independent choices: me / te / le / nos / os / les depends on the person; duele / duelen depends on the body part.
06 / Naming the person

A + name + pronoun + doler

When you explicitly name the person who feels the pain, introduce that person with a. Keep the matching indirect-object pronoun.

Correct pattern
A María le duele la cabeza.
María's head hurts.
A Juan le duele el estómago.
A Juan y Ana les duelen los pies.
Avoid these mistakes
✕María le duele la cabeza.
✕A María duele la cabeza.
✓A María le duele la cabeza.

Another possible order: Le duele la cabeza a María. The a + name phrase does not have to be first.

07 / Emphasis or clarification

A mí, a ti, a ella…

To emphasize or clarify the person, use a + a stressed pronoun. The corresponding short indirect-object pronoun is still required.

Emphatic phraseShort pronounExample
a mímeA mí me duele la cabeza.
a titeA ti te duele la cabeza.
a él / ella / ustedleA ella le duele la cabeza.
a nosotros/asnosA nosotros nos duele la cabeza.
a vosotros/asosA vosotros os duele la cabeza.
a ellos / ellas / ustedeslesA ellos les duele la cabeza.
Remember: Me duele la cabeza is already a complete sentence. Add a mí only when you want emphasis or clarification. A mí duele… and Yo me duele… are incorrect in this meaning.
08 / An important detail

Use the definite article

In this structure, Spanish normally uses el / la / los / las before body parts, rather than a possessive such as mi or mis.

Me duele la cabeza.
Le duele el estómago.
Te duelen los pies.
Nos duelen las muelas.
Natural in this context
Me duele la cabeza.
Normally avoid here
Me duele mi cabeza.
The pronoun me / te / le… already identifies whose body part is affected. The definite article agrees with the body-part noun.
09 / Comparing structures

Doler vs tener vs estar

These structures all describe health problems, but their grammatical subjects are not the same.

10 / Interactive practice

Build the correct sentence

Use the clues to make a complete sentence with doler. Say it aloud, then reveal the answer. Watch for a + person, the short pronoun, and duele / duelen.

Question 1 of 6
yo / la cabeza

Answers: 1. Me duele la cabeza. 2. Te duelen los pies. 3. A María le duele el estómago. 4. Nos duelen las muelas. 5. A Juan y Ana les duele la espalda. 6. Os duelen los oídos.

11 / Key takeaways

DOLER: four rules to remember

1

The body part is the subject.

La cabeza and los pies are grammatical subjects, not direct objects.

2

The verb agrees with the subject.

Duele + singular subject; duelen + plural subject.

3

Use an indirect-object pronoun for the person.

me, te, le, nos, os, les tells us who feels the pain.

4

Use a when you name the person.

A María le duele la cabeza. Keep the corresponding short pronoun.

A María le duele la cabeza.
