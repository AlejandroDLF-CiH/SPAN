Spanish Charades

How many Spanish actions can your team guess?

  1. Review the vocabulary.You will see 17 Spanish verbs and verb phrases. Read them together before the game.
  2. Work in pairs.One student is the actor; the other is the guesser.
  3. Take your positions.The actor faces the screen. The guesser faces the actor, with their back to the screen.
  4. Act and guess.The actor acts out the phrase without speaking or writing. The guesser says the phrase in Spanish.
  5. You have 5 seconds!Watch the countdown: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. The screen moves to the next phrase automatically.
  6. Earn points.Each correct phrase guessed before time runs out earns 1 point. The teacher keeps score.
Each pair plays one round. The team with the highest score wins!
